Шаги квеста

Текст шагов из английского клиента. Русский перевод подставим из клиента сервера.

1. Meet With Misa

Rafferty asks you to speak with a woman named Misa at Frost Lake. Your meeting is scheduled for after nightfall, so you must travel in the dark.

2. Letter for Rafforty

Misa tells you to give her letter to Rafforty.

3. Under Suspicion?

Rafforty is sorry that he doubted you.

4. Sudden Offer

Rafforty says that he belongs to the Zinnia Guild and asks for your help.

5. Momentary Wait

Rafforty says he has heard a different story, and asks you to come back later.

6. Go Back to Misa

Rafferty tells you that he has received new information from Misa. He asks you to meet with her after nightfall, so you'll have to travel in the dark.

7. In Search of the Ice Sculpture

Misa says the missing informants have already been turned into ice sculptures. Go and find them.

8. Whereabouts of Kierre

You have obtained the information about Kierre's whereabouts. Go to Kierre.

9. Go to Rafforty

You have obtained a report from Kierre, who now is an ice sculpture. Take the report to Rafforty.

10. Decypher the Cryptogram

Rafforty asks you to wait while he decodes the cryptogram.

11. In Search of Tablet Fragment

Rafforty needs the Tablet Fragments for his decryption spell. You remember seeing the fragments. Go to the place where the ice sculptures are located.

12. Go to Rafforty

You've found the Tablet Fragments. Return to Rafforty.
