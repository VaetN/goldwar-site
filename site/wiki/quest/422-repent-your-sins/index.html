Квест Repent Your Sins

с 1 ур. повторяемый

Repent Your Sins – повторяемый квест Lineage 2 Interlude с 1 уровня. Берётся у NPC Black Judge. По ходу нужно убить 4 вида монстров. Награда: Manacles of Penitent ×1.

Шаги квеста

Текст шагов из английского клиента. Русский перевод подставим из клиента сервера.

1. Sincerity of a Penitent's Heart

The Black Judge tells the sinner that he has a chance for redemption. Will you follow the path of penitence, sure to be filled with trials and tribulations?

2. Sincerity of a Penitent's Heart

The Black Judge wants to test your sincerity in seeking atonement for your sins. She asks you to go and help Katari and then return to her.

3. Sincerity of a Penitent's Heart

The Black Judge wants to test your sincerity in seeking atonement for your sins. She asks you to go and help Piotur and then return to her.

4. Sincerity of a Penitent's Heart

The Black Judge wants to test your sincerity in seeking atonement for your sins. She asks you to go and help Elder Casian and then return to her. (If you add or change your sub-class while fulfilling this quest, the quest will automatically be terminated.)

5. Sincerity of a Penitent's Heart

The Black Judge wants to test your sincerity in seeking atonement for your sins. She asks you to go and help Magister Joan and then return to her. (If you add or change your sub-class while fulfilling this quest, the quest will automatically be terminated.)

6. Help with the Missionary Work

Katari requests that you kill the scavenger wererats that have been disrupting her missionary work.

7. Help with the Farming

Piotur says that his farm is being attacked by Turek war hounds. He asks you to drive them away.

8. Help the Wastelands

Elder Casian asks you to bring him the heart of a tyrant kingpin.

9. Help with Spore Research

Magister Joan requests that you kill a trisalim tarantula and bring back its venom sac.

10. To the Black Judge

You have completed the task Katari requested. Return to the Black Judge.

11. To the Black Judge

You have completed the task requested by Piotur. Return to the Black Judge.

12. To the Black Judge

You have completed the task requested by Elder Casian. Return to the Black Judge.

13. To the Black Judge

You have completed the task requested by Magister Joan. Return to the Black Judge.

14. Make a Penitent's Manacle

The Black Judge asks you to go to Blacksmith Pushkin and make a Penitent's Manacle.

15. Toward Judgement

You asked Blacksmith Pushkin to make Penitent's Manacles. Return to the Black Judge.

16. Cleanse Your Own Sins

The Black Judge asks you to lock up the sin eater with a manacle of penitent and to care for him for the sake of cleansing your soul.
