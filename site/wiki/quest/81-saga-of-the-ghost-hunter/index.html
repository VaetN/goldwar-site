Квест Saga of the Ghost Hunter

с 1 ур.

Saga of the Ghost Hunter – квест Lineage 2 Interlude с 1 уровня.

Шаги квеста

Текст шагов из английского клиента. Русский перевод подставим из клиента сервера.

1. Forbidden Power

Black Cat, a venerable member of the guild, says that you must obtain the power that was forbidden by the gods. Meet Drunkard Master Drakon in the Town of Goddard to find out more.

2. Master Chef Donath

Master Drakon wants Donath's secret recipe book. Visit Master Chef Donath in the Hot Springs area.

3. Searching for Ice Crystals

Master Chef Donath asks you to find him some Ice Crystals that he will trade you for his cookbook. He suggests you talk to Chef Jeremy in the Hot Springs area.

4. Delivering the Cookbook

Chef Donath gives you his cookbook in exchange for the Ice Crystals. Take it to Master Drakon.

5. Seeking the Tablet of Vision - 1

Master Drakon says that you must obtain the forbidden power that is contained within the Tablets of Vision and tells you where to find the first Resonance Amulet and Tablet of Vision.

6. Seeking the Tablet of Vision - 2

At the end of the communion, the tablet tells you where to find the next Resonance Amulet. Get it from the Guardian of Forbidden Knowledge.

7. Commune With the Tablet of Vision - 2

You've obtained the Resonance Amulet from the Guardian of Forbidden Knowledge. Start communing with the second tablet.

8. Seeking the Tablet of Vision - 3

The tablet tells you where to find the third Resonance Amulet.

9. Commune With the Tablet of Vision - 3

Using the Resonance Amulet you took from Ezekiel, commune with the third tablet.

10. Mysterious Hermit - 1

The tablet shows a Human form. He appears to be somewhere between the Town of Goddard and the Ketra Orc Outpost. Find him.

11. The Hermit's Test

The hermit says that you must prove yourself before getting the Resonance Amulet, and orders you to get the Divine Stone of Wisdom from the Ketra Orcs. To do so, you must gain their trust and prove your wisdom. Meet Ketra's Messenger Wahkan.

12. The Hermit's Test

The hermit says that you must prove yourself before getting the Resonance Amulet and tells you to get the Divine Stone of Wisdom from the Varka Silenos. To do so, you must gain their trust and prove your wisdom. Speak with Naran Ashanuk, their messenger.

13. Seeking the Tablet of Vision - 4

The hermit gives you the Resonance Amulet and orders you to find the fourth Tablet of Vision and commune with it.

14. Mysterious Hermit - 2

The tablet shows a Human form and tells you where to find the next Resonance Amulet. It seems to be on the road between the Wall of Argos and the Town of Goddard.

15. Archon of Halisha

The hermit gives you another test to learn the location of the Tablet of Vision. He says that you must draw the Archon of Halisha out by attacking his minions northeast of the Wall of Argos, or confront him directly in the Imperial Tomb, kill him and take his Resonance Amulet. Monsters to Hunt - Shrine of Loyalty: Grave Scarabs, Scavenger Scarabs, Grave Ants, Scavenger Ants, Shrine Knights, Shrine Guards and the Archon of Halisha Imperial Tomb: Archon of Halisha

16. Seeking the Tablet of Vision - 5

You've killed the Archon of Halisha and obtained the Resonance Amulet you need to commune with the fifth Tablet of Vision. Find the fifth tablet the hermit told you about.

17. Hero vs. Archangel

When the communion with the fifth Tablet of Vision was nearly complete, it directed you to the sixth Tablet of Vision. Go there!

18. Commune With the Tablet of Vision -6

Aria Firstmatter gave you the Resonance Amulet when Monument Guardian Azrael ran away. Now commune with the last tablet.

19. Ghost Hunter

You've finished communing with all the Tablets of Vision. Return to the Black Cat and tell her what you've learned.

20. Towards Ghost Hunter

Black Cat tells you to train more to prepare yourself to use the power you've gained properly. Get some training and return to Black Cat.
