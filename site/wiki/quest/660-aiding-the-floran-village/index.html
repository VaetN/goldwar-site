1. The Woes of Floran Village

Floran Village needs help in their fight against their oppressive lord. Meet with Captain Alex in Floran Village.

2. Eliminate the Monsters in Floran Clearing

The Floran Village farmers cannot depend on the Territorial Army to protect them anymore. Defeat the monsters in the Floran Clearing and take evidence of your success to Captain Alex.
