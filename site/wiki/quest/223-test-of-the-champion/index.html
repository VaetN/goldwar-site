Шаги квеста

Текст шагов из английского клиента. Русский перевод подставим из клиента сервера.

1. The Old Soldier's Friend

Veteran Ascalon asks you to find his old friend Mason in the southern part of Dion.

2. Mason's Revenge

Mason's entire family was massacred by troops of the Gracian army. Exact his revenge upon the Bloody Axe Elites on the Plains of the Lizardmen. Monsters to Hunt - Bloody Axe Elites

3. Return to Mason

You've collected 100 heads of the Bloody Axe Elite. Now take them to Mason.

4. Letter to Ascalon

Mason is satisfied that his family has been avenged. He asks you to deliver a letter to Ascalon.

5. Poison of Medusa

Now you must make an antidote for the Giran guards who were poisoned by the medusa. Find Groot in Giran Castle Town.

6. An Antidote for Medusa Poisoning

It wasn't a mortal threat to the race, but the number medusa poisonings certainly increased. Create an antidote from a harpy's egg, the medusa's venom and windsus bile. Monsters to Hunt - Gorgon Flower Garden: Harpy, Medusa Death Pass: Windsus

7. Return to Groot

You've gathered the ingredients to make the antidote for the medusa's venom. Return to Magic Trader Groot.

8. Fight of the Old Soldiers

Groot thanks you for helping him and tells you that he will make and deliver the antidote. Now return to Ascalon.

9. Crisis In the Town of Oren

The Town of Oren is threatened! Meet Captain Mouen there!

10. Hunting the Road Scavenger

The crisis in Oren was a bit overblown by Ascalon, but the road scavengers are clearly causing trouble. Bring back 100 of their heads! Monsters to Hunt - Road Scavengers, Road Collectors

11. Return to Mouen

You've collected 100 heads of the road scavengers. Return to Captain Mouen.

12. Protect Giran Castle

Ascalon was right! the Leto Lizardmen have begun a full frontal assault! You must defend Giran Castle! Monsters to Hunt - Leto Lizardmen

13. Return to the Captain

You've collected 100 fangs of Leto Lizardmen. Return to Captain Mouen.

14. Return to Ascalon

You've saved Giran! Return to Veteran Ascalon.
