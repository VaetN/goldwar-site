Шаги квеста

Текст шагов из английского клиента. Русский перевод подставим из клиента сервера.

1. Meet Gabrielle

Bard Talien is collecting the records of ancient heroes. He has asked you to find a record regarding the seventeen heroes who confronted the Land Dragon Antharas. Go to Giran Castle Town and meet Gabrielle.

2. Antharas Watchman Gilmore

Gabrielle says that she doesn't know many heroes who have fought Antharas. She suggests that you meet Gilmore, Watchman of the Land Dragon Antharas, who was one of the seventeen heroes. He's become an undead and guards Dragon Valley.

3. Elven Witch Baraham, Creature of Evil

Elven Witch Baraham was one of the seventeen heroes who fought Antharas. She became an evil creature of her own free will. She must be carrying "Legend of Seventeen" in her bosom. Monster to hunt - Baraham

4. Go to Talien

Take the "Legend of Seventeen" that Baraham was carrying to Talien in Aden Castle Town.

5. Where is Melody Maestro?

It is rumored that a Melody Maestro in Heine has the echo crystal that contains a forgotten song of ancient bards regarding King Raul and his five knights. Go get the crystal.

6. Malruk Succubus Claw

Melody Maestro Kantabilon told you that in order to repair the old echo crystal, he needs a special tool. He asked you to go to Dragon Valley and obtain at least 10 Malruk Succubus Claws. Monsters to Hunt - Malruk Succubus and Malruk Succubus Turens

7. Go to the Melody Maestro

You've obtained 10 Malruk Succubus Claws! Now take them to Melody Maestro Kantabilon in Heine.

8. Take the Echo Crystal to Talien

Melody Maestro Kantabilon has given you the echo crystal containing the forgotten song. Return to Talien in Aden Castle Town.

9. An Elf whose name is Stedmiel

Go to Hunters Village and meet an Elf by the name of Stedmiel. Ask him about the poetry book containing the story of the Warriors who fought Beleth.

10. Take the Faded Poetry Book to Talien

Master Stedmiel gives you a poetry book in which the story of the warriors who fought against Beleth is written. Take it to Talien in Aden Castle Town.

11. Talien's Sponsor Virgil

Go see Dwarf Virgil, Talien's sponsor, in the Rune Township.

12. Meet Ogmar

Kassandra, Virgil's granddaughter, is suffering from an unknown illness. Ogmar, Virgil's colleague, may know a treatment for her illness. Seek his advice.

13. Grand Seer's Miracle Cure

Meet Grand Seer Rahorakti and ask him about some medicine for Kassandra, the sick Dwarven girl.

14. In Search of Crimson Moss

Grand Seer Rahorakti tells you that to make the medicine that Kassandra needs, he must have Crimson Moss. You can obtain it by hunting Splinter Stakato Drones in the Swamp of Screams. Retrive five of them. Monsters to Hunt - Splinter Stakatos, Splinter Stakato Walkers, Soldiers, Drones

15. Meet the Grand Seer

Take the Crimson Moss to Grand Seer Rahorakti.

16. Kassandra

Take the medicine Grand Seer Rahorakti gave you to Kassandra.

17. Return to Virgil

Virgil, Kassandra's grandfather, wants to see you.

18. Meet the Lady of the Lake

There is a gathering of people who worship the Lady of the Lake. Meet one member of that group, Caradine, in the Town of Goddard.

19. Meet Head Blacksmith Noel

Head Blacksmith Noel is currently restoring ancient weapons for a new generation of heroes. Go help him with the restoration work.

20. Obtain the Ingredients for Noel

Obtain the materials Noel needs to restore the ancient weapons. Collect five Lunargents and one bottle of Hellfire Oil. Magic Trader Wesley at the Ivory Tower will tell you how to obtain these materials.

21. Return to Caradine

You've provided Noel with all the materials he needs. Now, go see Caradine, the messenger of the lake.
