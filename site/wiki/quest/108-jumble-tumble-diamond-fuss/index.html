Квест Jumble, Tumble, Diamond Fuss

с 10 ур.

Jumble, Tumble, Diamond Fuss – квест Lineage 2 Interlude с 10 уровня. Берётся у NPC Gouph. По ходу нужно убить 3 вида монстров. Награда: Echo Crystal - Theme of Battle ×10, Echo Crystal - Theme of Love ×10, Echo Crystal - Theme of Solitude ×10, Echo Crystal - Theme of the Feast ×10.

Шаги квеста

Текст шагов из английского клиента. Русский перевод подставим из клиента сервера.

1. In Search of a Star Diamond!

Collector Gouph of the Bronze Key Guild has purchased a rare star diamond from Trader Reep of the Silver Scales Guild. It is one of only twelve on the continent! Three days have past since the agreed-upon delivery date and Gouph is becoming impatient. He asks you to go to Trader Reep, retrieve the star diamond and bring it to him.

2. Carrier Torocco

Trader Reep informs you that he gave the star diamond to Carrier Torocco to deliver to Gouph. Find Torocco and ask him about the diamond.

3. Booze can make you lose!

Torocco got drunk with Miner Maron last night and passed out. When he awoke, his weapons, armor, money and even the star diamond had been stolen! Find Maron and ask him what happened.

4. Gambling can make you a loser, too!

Carrier Torocco is usually quite trustworthy, but.when he starts drinking he inevitably starts gambling. He got drunk last night and lost everything he had in a dice game with Blacksmith Brunon. Could this mean that Blacksmith Brunon has the star diamond?

5. Brunon's Request

Indeed, Blacksmith Brunon won the star diamond in a dice game! He tells you that he will give it to you if you recover the aquamarine and chrysoberyl that were stolen by a goblin brigand lieutenant.

6. Return to Blacksmith Brunon

You have recovered the aquamarines and chrysoberyls that Blacksmith Brunon lost. Take them to him.

7. Gem Box

Brunon gives you the gem box he says he won in a dice game. Take it to Gouph.

8. Useless Coal

The gem box doesn't contain the star diamond, only a strange looking piece of coal! Return to Blacksmith Brunon and demand an explanation!

9. A Chest of Mistaken Identity

The piece of coal once belonged to Warehouse Freightman Murdoc. Take the letter from Brunon to Murdoc and exchange it for the gem box that contains the star diamond.

10. The Diamond Is Gone!

The star diamond was eaten by a bat that Warehouse Keeper Airy keeps as a pet! The bat escaped out of an open window! Go quickly and speak with Airy!

11. Who keeps a bat as a pet, anyway?

Warehouse Keeper Airy asks you to find her pet, a blade bat. Kill the bat and get the diamond.

12. Small Sacrifice, Great Reward

You finally recover the star diamond. Pray for the soul of the poor bat then take the star diamond to Collector Gouph.
