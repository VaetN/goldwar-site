Квест Saga of the Spectral Dancer

с 1 ур.

Saga of the Spectral Dancer – квест Lineage 2 Interlude с 1 уровня.

Шаги квеста

Текст шагов из английского клиента. Русский перевод подставим из клиента сервера.

1. Meet Elena

Mordred, The Dark Knight tells you to visit Spellbook Seller Elena in the Town of Goddard.

2. In Search of Bavarin

Spellbook Seller Elena tells you to visit Bavarin and borrow the "Ancient Language Dictionary" from him.

3. Return with the Ice Crystals

Information Broker Bavarin says he'll let you borrow the "Ancient Language Dictionary" if you get him some Ice Crystals to soothe his arthritis. To get the Ice Crystals, visit Chef Jeremy in the Hot Springs area.

4. Return to Elena

When you deliver the Ice Crystals, Information Broker Bavarin gladly lends the "Ancient Language Dictionary" to you. Take it to Spellbook Seller Elena.

5. Go to the Tablet of Vision

Spellbook Seller Elena hands the Resonance Amulet over to you, telling you to go to the Tablet of Vision.

6. In Search of the Second Resonance Amulet

According to the first Tablet of Vision, you'll find the person with the Resonance Amulet near the next tablet.

7. Towards the Tablet of Vision

You've got the second Resonance Amulet. Go to the Tablet of Vision and try communing with it.

8. In Search of the Third Resonance Amulet

At the second tablet, you saw who has the Resonance Amulet near the third tablet. To find him, commune with the third tablet.

9. Towards the Tablet of Vision

You've got the third Resonance Amulet. Go to the Tablet of Vision and try communing with it.

10. Meet Elena

At the third tablet, you learned that Spellbook Seller Elena of the Town of Goddard has a Resonance Amulet. Visit her and get the Resonance Amulet.

11. In Search of Ketra Orcs

Priest Bastian tells you to gain the trust of Ketra's Messenger Wahkan and get the Divine Stone of Wisdom.

12. In Search of Varka Silenos

Priest Bastian tells you to gain the trust of Varka's Messenger Naran Ashanuk and get the Divine Stone of Wisdom.

13. Towards the Tablet of Vision

You've got the Divine Stone of Wisdom and made a new Resonance Amulet. Now, go to the Tablet of Vision and try communing with it.

14. Meet Elena

The location of the next Resonance Amulet is not shown. Visit Elena and ask for help.

15. In Search of the Resonance Amulet

Spellbook Seller Elena tells you that if you go north from the fork in road, you'll get to the Shrine of Loyalty. If you go east from there, you'll find the Imperial Tomb. Monsters to Hunt - Shrine of Loyalty: Grave Scarabs, Scavenger Scarabs, Grave Ants, Scavenger Ants, Shrine Knights, Shrine Guards and the Archon of Halisha Imperial Tomb: the Archon of Halisha

16. Towards the Tablet of Vision

You've got the fifth Resonance Amulet. Go to the Tablet of Vision and try communing with it.

17. In Search of the Last Resonance Amulet

You feel that the last Resonance Amulet is near the tablet. First, go to the tablet.

18. Towards the Last Tablet of Vision

You've defeated Death Lord Hallate and taken his Resonance Amulet. Now, try the last communion.

19. Finishing the Journey

The long journey has ended. Report your accomplishments to Mordred, The Dark Knight.

20. Finishing the Journey

Train some more before you return to Mordred, The Dark Knight at Hardin's Academy. (Only characters of level 76 or above may change their occupation to Spectral Dancer. Don't abandon the quest until occupation change is complete.)
