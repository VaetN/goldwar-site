Шаги квеста

Текст шагов из английского клиента. Русский перевод подставим из клиента сервера.

1. Meet Ossian

Ask Ossian what you need to do to restore the strength of the Lady of the Lake.

2. Oppressors of Splendor

Recover the Ring of Goddess: Waterbinder and Necklace of Goddess: Evergreen. The ring can be obtained from a Pilgrim of Splendor and the necklace can be obtained from a Judge of Splendor. Monsters to Hunt - Pilgrims of Splendor and Judges of Splendor

3. Recovered Ring and Necklace

You've recovered the Ring of Goddess: Waterbinder and the Necklace of Goddess: Evergreen. Take them to Ossian in the Town of Goddard.

4. Flame of Splendor, Barakiel

Go to the Valley of Saints and confront Barakiel, the Flame of Splendor. Slay him and recover the Rain Song, Staff of the Goddess.

5. Take the Rain Song to Ossian

Take Rain Song, the Staff of the Goddess, to Ossian in the Town of Goddard.

6. To the Ivory Tower

Deliver the three sacred relics of the goddess to Magister Ladd at the Ivory Tower. He's discovered a way to tap the sacred power contained within them.
