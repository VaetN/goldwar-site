Шаги квеста

Текст шагов из английского клиента. Русский перевод подставим из клиента сервера.

1. Find the Elrokian Chief Mushika

They say the Elroki tribe, native to the Primeval Isle, possesses a mysterious skill which allows its people to effortlessly hunt dinosaurs. Find the Elrokian chief and ask him about that skill.

2. Find the Chief's Son

The Elrokian chief Mushika is gravely ill, and it is difficult to speak with him. Speak with his son instead.

3. Return to Marquez

You speak with Asamah, the chief's son, but he turns you away because you are an outsider. Return to Marquez and find a way to win the friendship of the Elroki tribe.

4. Retrieve the Diary Fragments from the Velociraptors

To become a friend of the Elroki tribe, you must know their traditional song. To learn it, defeat the Velociraptors that killed the expedition member who was searching for clues about the song and retrieve the fragments of his diary. The Velociraptors are said to roam the Primeval Isle's "Lost Nest." Monster to Hunt: Velociraptor.

5. Take the Diary Fragments to Marquez

You have collected all the diary fragments. Return to Marquez.

6. Find Hunter Kirikachin

According to the diary, an old Elrokian hunter named Kirikachin may be willing to teach the song to an outsider. Find him, deliver the letter and learn the song.

7. Kirikachin's Sorrow

After reading the letter, Kirikachin seems very sad. Comfort him and learn the song.

8. Song of the Warrior

You have learned the sad but proud Elrokian tune, the "Warrior's Song." Go to Asamah, the chief's son, and sing it to him.

9. Friend of the Elroki Tribe

You sing before Asamah, and all the Elrokians nearby join in the Warrior's Song. The act wins you the friendship of the Elroki tribe, and Asamah is now willing to speak with you.

10. Practice Trap Parts

As a friend of the Elroki tribe, you can now learn their mysterious skill. To prepare to learn it, hunt the following dinosaurs and bring back the parts and ingredients needed to craft the perfect trap. Monsters to Hunt: Ornithomimus, Deinonychus, Pachycephalosaurus.

11. Deliver Trap Parts to Asamah

You have collected all the parts necessary to create the perfect trap. Return to Asamah.

12. Learn Trapping Skills from Kirikachin

You have received a trap from Asamah. Go to Kirikachin to learn how to use it.
